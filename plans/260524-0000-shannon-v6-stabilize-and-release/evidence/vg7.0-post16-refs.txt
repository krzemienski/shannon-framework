396:## The Shannon Insight
398:Claude Shannon proved you can transmit information reliably over a noisy channel by adding redundant encoding. Same principle applies here. A single instruction in CLAUDE.md is a signal in a noisy channel. Long context, competing priorities, complex task chains. By the time the agent is deep in an implementation, that instruction is noise.
